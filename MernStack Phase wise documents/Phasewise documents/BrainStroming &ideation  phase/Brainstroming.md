
# Phase 1: Brainstorming & Ideation Phase - HouseRent Portal

## 1. Problem Statement
Finding a rental house or listing a property for rent is currently a chaotic process. Renters face issues with fake listings, high brokerage fees, and lack of direct communication with owners. On the other hand, property owners struggle to find verified tenants, track monthly rent payments, and manage property bookings efficiently.

## 2. Proposed Solution
The *HouseRent Portal* is a unified MERN stack application that directly connects property owners and renters, eliminating middlemen. 
* It provides a dedicated *Admin Dashboard* to verify users and listings.
* An *Owner Dashboard* to post properties, manage bookings, and track rent status.
* A *Renter Dashboard* to search, filter, and book houses seamlessly.

## 3. Target Audience
* *College Students & Working Professionals:* Looking for affordable shared or private spaces.
* *Families:* Looking for long-term residential houses.
* *Property Owners / Landlords:* Who want to rent out apartments, independent houses, or rooms.
* *System Administrators:* Who moderate listings and users for safety.

## 4. Key Value Propositions (USP)
* *Zero Brokerage:* Direct peer-to-peer connection.
* *Role-based Dashboards:* Dedicated experiences for Admin, Owner, and Renter.
* *Instant Verification:* Spam listings are blocked by the admin.
* *Real-time Notifications:* Reusable Toast systems for status updates.
